Your Trusted Partner for Aluminium & Glass

Premium Aluminium and Glass Ltd

Established in 2011, Premium Aluminium and Glass Ltd (RC: 1497269) is a leading provider of fabrication, supply, and installation of aluminium and glass works in Nigeria. With over a decade of experience, we have successfully delivered 300+ projects across residential and commercial sectors.

Our team of skilled professionals is dedicated to delivering innovative, high-quality solutions that meet the unique needs of each client. From architectural glass systems to custom aluminium frames, we combine technical expertise with superior materials to ensure lasting results.

What We Do

Our Specialized Services

Aluminium Windows & Doors

Sliding & casement windows, shop front systems, office door frames, and protective screens designed for durability and style.

Curtain Walls

Frameless glass walls, spider wall fittings, structural glazed systems, and architectural support for modern facades.

Cladding (ACP Walls)

ACP wall panels, exterior finishing, weather-resistant panels, and custom designs for enhanced building aesthetics.

Partitions & Ceilings

Single & double glazed partitions, frameless glass partitions, suspended ceilings, and office dividers.

Hand Railings

Stainless steel railings, glass staircase supports, balcony railings, and custom designs for safety and elegance.

Specialized Glass Installations

Frameless glass & spider walls, skylights, customized shower cubicles, and architectural glass systems.

High-Security & Automatic Systems

Automatic sliding & revolving doors, bulletproof & blast proof windows, laminated anti-vandal glass, and secure access solutions.

Roofing Installation

Metal & glass roofs, transparent roof systems, light-permeable designs, and weather-resistant structures.

Project Support & Consultancy

Technical consultancy, aluminium product design, shop drawings, material supply & supervision, and staff training.
